Emergency Water Damage Restoration Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Wrap plumbing in cold, unheated parts of the house so a hard freeze cannot crack it.
  • Know where your electrical panel is so power to wet rooms can be cut safely.
  • Going away longer than a few days? Close the main water valve first.
  • Sinks, dishwashers, washing machines and water heaters are all good spots for a leak sensor.
  • A sump pump should be tried out before storms arrive, ideally with a backup battery installed.
  • Important paperwork and valuables belong above floor level, most of all on the lowest story.

Signs It Is Time for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Several hours or more have passed with hardwood, drywall or carpet sitting wet.
  • Gallons keep spilling from a ruptured water heater tank.
  • You have no way to locate or get to the shutoff while water continues to pour.
  • Pooled water is creeping over the floor into neighboring rooms or onto the staircase.
  • You came home to water and do not know how long it ran.
  • A leak upstairs is sending water down the walls of the level beneath it.
  • You see water escaping around a light fitting, from a wall outlet or through the ceiling.
