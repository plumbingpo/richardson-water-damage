Water Heater Leak Cleanup Help in Richardson

The tank on a water heater stores 40 to 80 gallons, yet a leak can release far more than that. Your supply line keeps feeding the tank, which means it fills back up just as quickly as water escapes through a rusted bottom. Until someone closes the cold water valve, it keeps running. How bad it gets depends on placement, whether that is the garage, a closet in the hall or an attic sitting above finished rooms, which is the worst.

After extracting the water, our Richardson crew checks the materials that sat next to the unit. The platform beneath the tank and the base of the closet walls are usually soaked. Garage leaks wet whatever is stored on the floor, along with the wall the garage shares with the house. Attic leaks travel downward through the insulation and the ceilings below. We measure each area and dry it with air movers and dehumidifiers.

Do This First

What to Do Right Now

  1. Cut the Power or GasFor an electric heater, switch off its breaker. On a gas unit, rotate the gas control to its off setting. Never leave a heating element or burner running on an empty tank.
  2. Close the Cold SupplyThe cold line over the tank has a valve, and it closes with a clockwise turn. If it is stuck or leaking, shut off the main water valve for the house.
  3. Watch for Hot WaterWhat comes out of the tank can cause scalds. Pets and children should stay clear, and nobody should walk into pooled water before it cools down.
  4. Empty the Tank OutsideA garden hose on the drain valve, if you can get to it safely, lets you send what is left in the tank outside or into a floor drain instead of across the floor.
  5. Move Stored ItemsLift boxes, rugs and anything absorbent off the wet floor. Fabric and cardboard absorb water fast, then keep it pressed to the concrete and walls.
Insurance

Does Insurance Cover It?

Water damage to belongings, walls and floors from a tank that fails suddenly is often covered as a sudden and accidental loss. The water heater itself usually is not, since wearing out with age is considered maintenance. If an adjuster finds signs of a long-term leak, such as rust trails or old staining, the claim can be reduced or denied because gradual leaks usually are not covered. Read your policy, take photos of the damage and the heater, and do not dispose of the old unit before the claim is settled.

Cost

What Affects the Cost

  • How much insulation, ceiling or wall drywall needs to be taken out.
  • The location of the heater, since a garage leak costs less than one in an interior closet or attic.
  • Time that passed with the tank still refilling, up to the point the supply was shut.
  • Whether flooring in adjoining rooms was reached and what type it is.

Why It Happens

What Causes It

  • Connections that were not fully tightened during a repair or a new installation.
  • The drain valve at the bottom of the tank cracking, loosening or failing to shut fully.
  • Rust at the connections above the tank, either the flex lines or the inlet and outlet fittings.
  • Pressure rising too high in the tank because the thermostat failed or no expansion tank was installed.
  • Sediment and corrosion that eat through the bottom of an aging steel tank.
What Is at Risk

What Happens if You Wait

Ceilings Under an Attic Heater

Water from an attic unit runs down through insulation to the ceiling beneath it. Insulation that is wet no longer insulates, and the added weight can pull the ceiling drywall down.

Garage Wall Base

Water crossing a garage slab soaks the bottom plate and drywall of the wall shared with the house, then moves into the flooring on the living side.

Items Stored Near the Heater

Whatever is kept by a closet or garage heater, from keepsakes to furniture and boxes, is the first thing to get wet. Mold can develop on damp contents that are not promptly dried or tossed, while cardboard boxes disintegrate.

Adjacent Room Flooring

Water leaving a heater closet runs under the door and into carpet, laminate or hardwood in the hall. Because the water is hot, wood floors swell faster and the backing on carpet can let go.
