Storm Preparation for Homeowners in Richardson, TX

Home / Blog / Preparing Your Home for Storms in Richardson, TX

Homes and commercial buildings across Richardson, TX can take severe water damage when storms hit. You can greatly limit what a storm does to your property, and the restoration bill that follows, by preparing once before the season starts and again as each storm approaches. This checklist from Richardson Restoration covers everything Richardson homeowners should do to prepare.

Pre-Season Preparation

Ahead of the season, do the following: check the roof for flashing or shingles that are missing, damaged, or worn out. Clean gutters and downspouts thoroughly and ensure proper discharge. Go around all exterior penetrations, doors, and windows, checking and sealing as you go. Inspect your sump pump and test the backup power system. Read through your coverage so you know where it stands on flood damage and wind damage. Take up-to-date photos of the property to keep with your insurance records. Cut back trees close to the house so wind-blown branches do less damage.

When a Storm Is on the Way

When storms are forecast: Secure loose outdoor items that could become projectiles. Close storm shutters or board windows if warranted. Ensure your sump pump is operational and the battery backup is charged. Move valuables and important documents to upper levels or waterproof containers. Know your evacuation route and have supplies ready. Program Richardson Restoration's emergency number ((510) 447-5373) into your phone.

Once the Storm Has Passed

After the storm passes: Check for damage from a safe distance before entering. Record every bit of damage on video and in photos. If you can do it safely, tarp over any openings in the roof. Do not enter flooded areas until electrical hazards are assessed. Report the damage to your insurance company.
