How Much Does Water Damage Cost in Richardson?

Home / Blog / How Much Does Water Damage Cost in Richardson?

The Short Answer

Typical Richardson water damage projects cost from $1,500 up to $10,000 or more. With clean water and a small area, the total can stay below about $1,500. At the other end, heavy flooding or sewage with reconstruction work can go well beyond $10,000.

None of this is a quote, only the usual range across the country. Pricing in Richardson, TX depends on local wages and the going rate for materials, so your number may differ. For a precise figure, there is no substitute for moisture readings taken during an on-site visit.

Pricing by Category of Water

Restoration pricing usually starts with the type of water involved. There are three categories in the industry standard, and each reflects a level of contamination. As contamination rises, so does the need for cleaning, tear-out of materials and protective equipment.

Nationally, the category puts cleanup at about $3 to $7.50 for each square foot. Here are the usual numbers.

CategoryWhat it isTypical cost per sq ft
Category 1, clean waterSupply lines, faucets, water heater tanksRoughly $3 to $4
Category 2, gray waterWashing machine or dishwasher discharge, sump pump failuresAbout $4 to $6.50
Category 3 (black water)Sewage, river flooding, storm surgeRoughly $7 to $7.50

Category can change. After about two days of sitting, water that started clean carries bacteria and is reclassified to gray or black. It is part of why delay makes a job more expensive.

What Drives the Price

Two homes with the same broken pipe can get very different bills. These are the items a contractor weighs in an estimate.

  • Area affected. A larger wet area takes extra crew hours, additional machines and a longer stay.
  • Water category. Clean water is the cheapest to handle. Sewage is the most expensive.
  • How long it sat. Given time, water works its way into wall cavities and subfloors. Catching a leak within an hour is much cheaper than discovering it when you return from a weekend away.
  • Materials. Tile and concrete dry well. Insulation, drywall, carpet pad and hardwood frequently have to be replaced or dried with special methods.
  • Mold. If growth has started, mold remediation is added to the scope, with containment and air filtration.
  • Reconstruction. Drying is one cost. Afterward comes a second cost for new flooring, drywall, paint and cabinets, which on larger jobs often exceeds the first.

Most estimates are built around two phases: water extraction first, then structural drying. Drying usually takes 3 to 5 days.

A daily rate is common for dehumidifiers and air movers. A home that dries in three days costs less than one that takes six.

Price Ranges by Type of Job

These are typical national ranges for the jobs we see most often in Richardson. Use the numbers to get oriented and not as a firm price.

  • Burst pipe. Often $1,500 to $5,000 for cleanup and drying, plus the burst pipe repair itself. The total climbs when water flowed for hours or reached a second floor.
  • Appliance leak. Found early, a leak from a dishwasher, water heater or washing machine is often $1,000 to $4,000. Hidden damage makes a slow leak beneath a cabinet more expensive.
  • Basement flood. The usual range is $2,000 to $10,000 or more, varying with the water's source, its depth and any finished surfaces. Costs are highest for basement water removal when the space is finished.
  • Sewage backup. A common range is $3,000 to $10,000 or higher. Anything porous has to go and all surfaces get disinfected, since cleaning up sewage counts as Category 3.
  • Roof leak. Interior work on wet ceilings, walls and insulation often comes to $1,000 to $5,000, and the roof fix is extra.

Is Water Damage in Richardson Covered by Insurance?

It depends on the cause and on your policy. A typical homeowners policy often covers accidental events that happen suddenly, for example a pipe bursting or a supply line giving out with no warning.

Coverage usually does not extend to gradual leaks. Months of dripping from a fitting may be labeled a maintenance issue by the insurer. Outside flooding, for instance from storm surge or a river that rises, is usually not covered without separate flood insurance. A backed-up sewer often takes an added endorsement.

Before assuming anything, read the policy or phone your agent. The deductible is a factor as well. If the work is minor, its cost may be below your deductible and there may be little point in a claim.

Ways to Lower the Cost

How much you pay for water damage is largely settled within a day or two. Some basic actions go a long way.

  • Act fast. Water left in place goes on spreading. Each additional hour soaks more of the building.
  • Shut off the water. Learn the location of the main valve ahead of time, and turn it off the moment a leak shows up.
  • Document everything. Before moving or discarding anything, record it in photos and on video. Keep receipts.
  • Call a pro before mold starts. Within 24 to 48 hours, mold growth can get underway. A job with active growth takes more days and costs more money.
  • Move what you can. If you can do it safely, lift boxes, furniture and rugs clear of the wet floor.

Do not walk through standing water if the power is on, and stay out of anything that may contain sewage. The risk of being shocked or getting sick outweighs anything you might save. If the water came from outside, leave flood cleanup to a crew with the right protective gear.

Turning a Range Into an Estimate

Averages from across the country are fine for planning and still cannot describe your home. An inspection with a moisture meter shows how far the water travelled and what it will take to dry and repair.

If you need water damage restoration in Richardson, TX, the best time to ask for that inspection is now, while the damage is still small.
