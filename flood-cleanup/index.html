A sump pump that quits, a sewer that backs up, a pipe that lets go, rising river water, storm surge or a hard rain can each flood a property, and the damage should be handled by professionals without delay. In nearly every case the water falls into Category 2, known as gray water, or Category 3, known as black water, which means contaminants, bacteria and other hazards that are a real health risk. Day or night, 24/7, Richardson Restoration sends flood cleanup crews throughout Richardson to remove floodwater, sanitize the affected rooms and safely restore your home or building.

The first job is getting the water out quickly, and our submersible and truck-mounted pumps are able to clear thousands of gallons every hour. After that, IICRC-certified technicians on our team measure how contaminated the area is, then sanitize it with antimicrobial products, HEPA-filtered air cleaning and targeted disinfection of all surfaces the water reached. Materials beyond saving, like contaminated drywall, damaged insulation and saturated carpet pad, are removed and disposed of the right way.

Then the drying begins: high-velocity air movers and commercial dehumidifiers of the LGR (low-grain refrigerant) type go in to pull moisture out of the building's structural parts. We check moisture levels every day with calibrated meters and thermal imaging so the drying is complete and mold does not get a start. Every stage of the job is recorded for your claim: photographs, detailed moisture maps, logs of the equipment used and the scope-of-work reports that insurance adjusters require.

Why Flood Cleanup Is Not a DIY Job

Getting the standing water out is only one part of cleaning up after a flood. Chemicals, bacteria, sewage and other contamination ride in with the flood and create real health dangers. Protecting your family from E. coli, hepatitis and similar waterborne illness takes proper containment, correct use of protective equipment (PPE) and proven sanitizing methods, all things professional teams train for.

Improper cleanup also tends to miss moisture you cannot see, and within days that trapped water can cause a major mold problem. Our technicians rely on psychrometric calculations, meter readings and thermal imaging to verify that each affected spot, from the framing to the subfloor to the inside of the walls, meets accepted drying standards. Nothing else reliably heads off secondary damage, and that damage can run thousands more to remediate, so we dry by the numbers.

When to Call for Flood Cleanup in Richardson

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Flooring that squelches when you walk on it and walls damp to the touch
  • Water pooled on any floor of the building, no matter where it came from
  • Stains along baseboards and lower walls that mark the flood level
  • A sewer or drain backup pushing contaminated water into living spaces
  • Seepage at door thresholds, window wells or cracks in the foundation
  • Soaked landscaping that leaves water pooling against your foundation
  • Rainwater getting inside through a roof or wall damaged by a storm

Maintenance Tips for Flood Cleanup

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Use waterproof bins and raised shelving for anything valuable kept at ground level or below
  • Elevate appliances and electrical systems in flood-prone parts of your property
  • Regrade low spots so soil around the whole foundation falls away from the walls
  • Extend your downspouts to carry rain from the roof out past the foundation
  • A backwater valve on your sewer line keeps a backup from pushing sewage indoors
  • Seal foundation cracks and apply a waterproofing coating to basement walls
